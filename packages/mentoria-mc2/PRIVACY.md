# MentorIA MC2 privacy notice

Updated 16 November 2026. This notice covers MentorIA MC2 0.1.8 and
its optional personal contact form.

## In the AI chat

MentorIA uses the relevant text you share in the current chat to explore app
ideas, compare options and prepare a project brief. This can include your
professional role, business or course, audience, method and plans. The text
can include personal information if you choose to share it. Share only what
is needed and remove names or other identifying details about other people.

The skills are instructed to use the current chat and leave saved memory,
other chats, files and attachments out of the workflow. They have no
connectors or independent storage, and the package sends no chat data to
Mikel or an external service. The AI platform processes chat messages under
its own policies and account settings, which govern chat history, retention
and deletion. See the privacy notices for [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)
and [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/).

## Optional contact with Mikel

On request, MentorIA can produce an editable summary in the chat and show a
link to the contact form on mikelmc2.com. You decide what to copy into the
form and submit it yourself. When you submit, Innova Experience S.L. uses your
name, email address and chosen summary to handle and reply to your request.
SnakeMember records the contact in the list for your selected language and
sends Mikel the notification through SparkPost. The form uses Google
reCAPTCHA to prevent abuse.

Website requests are kept while they are handled and, if a relationship
follows, for its duration, subject to applicable legal retention periods.
The website notice explains the processors, retention and your data rights.
For website data requests, contact info@innovaxp.com.
[Read the website privacy notice for MentorIA](https://mikelmc2.com/privacidad#mentoria-contact).
