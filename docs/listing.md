## en

**MentorIA MC2**

Find the app in your method

AI-guided mentoring by Michele Cumer for experts, educators and course creators. Discover which app could help your audience apply your method, compare ideas and define a focused first version, including simpler alternatives when appropriate.

MentorIA uses relevant user and business context your AI host already makes available, together with what you share. Conversation and project briefs follow your language, including English, Italian and Spanish. It provides planning guidance, not app development, verified market demand or guaranteed business results.

The AI mentoring is complete on its own. If you ask for personal review or implementation help, MentorIA can prepare an editable summary and point you to Mikel's separate contact form in your language. You choose what to share, copy and paste it, and submit it yourself. Contact does not automatically include a free review, book a session or purchase a service.

This skills-only plugin has no MCP server, external login, account access or connection to Grimory or SnakeMember. It does not send the summary or save business profiles. The optional website form has its own privacy notice and processing; your AI host's data rules also apply.
