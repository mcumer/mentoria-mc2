# Optional personal help — product configuration

This resource configures MentorIA MC2's optional continuation with Mikel
(Michele Cumer). It is product information, not a required phase of the method.
The AI mentoring is useful on its own. Mention this route when the person asks
for personal review, mentoring or implementation help; do not add a sales pitch
to every result or redirect ordinary AI questions to a human.

## Prepare a shareable summary

Reuse what is already available. Include only relevant details the person
chooses to share: their method or course, intended audience, app opportunity
or current options, proposed interaction, smallest version if already chosen,
assumptions and open questions, and what they want Mikel's help with. A person
can ask for help while still exploring; do not invent a decision or require a
finished project. Keep the summary concise and editable, approximately 150–300
words when the available information justifies it. Unknowns remain unknown.

Exclude unrelated memories, private instructions, credentials and identifiable
customer or student data. Use anonymous examples. Do not copy the full chat.
Do not ask for name, email or other contact details in the AI conversation:
the person enters those on the website. Ask them to review the text before
sharing. Generating a summary does not transmit it or establish consent to
anything else.

## Verified destinations and copy

For Italian, Spanish or English use the matching link below. For another
language, keep the summary in that language and disclose that the English form
is the available fallback. The site's language switch allows another choice.
Use the fixed URL exactly: no summary, email, tracking identifier or other
personal data in query parameters or fragments. Do not browse or submit the
form on the person's behalf, call an API, or send email.

## Expectations

Personal review or implementation support is optional. Do not promise a free
review, acceptance, a response time, capacity, prices, deliverables or a booking
link. Those are agreed separately with Mikel. The website processes the explicit
request under its own privacy notice, using SnakeMember for the contact and
notification. This skills-only plugin has no connection to that system and
cannot confirm whether a request was submitted or an email was delivered.
